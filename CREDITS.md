# Season Shift — Credits & Licenses

All 3D models, textures, shaders, sound effects, and gameplay code in this
build were created originally for Season Shift. No paid assets were used.
The game is fully original and does not copy Temple Run (or any other
runner) names, art, or assets.

## Music

- Season theme tracks (`assets/music/spring.mp3`, `summer.mp3`, `autumn.mp3`,
  `winter.mp3`) were created for Season Shift with Suno, from prompts by
  Joseph, and are used with his permission:
  - Spring — smooth jazz piano, instrumental
  - Summer — Beach Boys style with a little reggae, instrumental
  - Autumn — big band, Frank Sinatra style, instrumental
  - Winter — lofi grooves with crackling vinyl, guitar, synth and a trap kit, instrumental

## Everything else

Penguin hero (Pip), hoodie costumes, trees, terrain, skies, coins, gates,
obstacles, HUD, and WebAudio sound effects are original procedural work
created for this game. No Kenney, Quaternius, or other third-party 3D packs
were required for this build.
